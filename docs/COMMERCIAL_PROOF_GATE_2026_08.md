# Farm Loop — Commercial Proof Gate

Last updated: 2026-08-24
Status: CONDITIONAL GO / validation in progress
Scope: Mobile-first cozy management / farming-adjacent / Japanese snow-country satoyama

## 0. Why this gate exists

Do not continue feature accumulation until Farm Loop proves four things:
1. Demand exists for the market position.
2. The product has a legible differentiator beyond generic farming.
3. The first 10–15 minutes are fun enough to produce voluntary continuation.
4. The product can be completed by a small/AI-assisted team without requiring Township-scale LiveOps or content volume.

This gate precedes v1.7 feature work.

## 2. Positioning hypothesis

### Working positioning
**A cozy management game where you restore and circulate a Japanese snow-country satoyama through farming, mountain work, village relationships, seasons, and ecology.**

Japanese shorthand:
**雪国の里山を循環させて育てる、Cozy Management Game。**

### What Farm Loop is NOT
- A Hay Day clone.
- A Township-scale city/farm LiveOps game.
- A generic crop collection game.
- A realistic agriculture simulator.
- A card-heavy management dashboard.

### Differentiation pillars
1. **Snow-country satoyama** — snow, mountain, creek, forest, village, seasonal visual transformation.
2. **Circular ecology** — chicken -> manure -> compost -> sansai -> bees/pollination -> mushrooms/processing/sales.
3. **Mountain work** — gathering routes and discovery are as important as the farm.
4. **Village relationships** — residents create human purpose for production.
5. **Seasonal anticipation** — players should want to see the next month/season.

If external users do not spontaneously perceive at least two of these pillars, differentiation fails.

## 5. Player-voice signals captured so far

### Strong warning cluster from mature F2P farming
Recent Hay Day community posts repeatedly mention:
- too many simultaneous events
- production tasks taking too long
- game feeling like work
- reward effort mismatch
- FOMO / repeated logins
- loss of calm routine

Township community posts similarly mention:
- repetitive progression
- Match-3/resource blockers
- perceived monetization pressure
- loss of motivation after repeated cycles

### Transfer to Farm Loop
Hard product principles:
1. No energy system for core actions at launch.
2. No mandatory repeated logins every 30–120 minutes.
3. No overlapping event stack that converts cozy play into obligation.
4. No single-item repeated chores when batch action preserves the decision.
5. Rewards must be recognized immediately and feel proportional to effort.
6. Optional goals must remain optional.
7. A session should have a natural stopping point.

## 6. Monetization hypothesis v1

### Primary hypothesis
**Premium one-time purchase** for the base game, with possible optional substantial expansion/DLC later.

Why this is currently preferred:
- Fits offline/single-player cozy management.
- Avoids FOMO/energy/paywall pressure that conflicts with the positioning.
- Reduces small-team LiveOps burden.
- Stardew Valley and Animal Crossing: Pocket Camp C provide current mobile evidence that paid games can remain visible in charts.
- Farm Loop's differentiator is world/experience depth, not competitive monetized progression.

### Explicitly rejected for initial launch
- forced ads
- energy/stamina monetization
- pay-to-skip core waiting
- battle pass
- high-frequency limited events
- gacha

### Still to validate
- target launch price
- willingness-to-pay
- premium vs free demo + paid unlock
- expansion economics

This is a hypothesis, not a final commercial commitment.

## 8. Product proof targets

### First 10–15 minute vertical slice must prove
Player can understand and enjoy:
1. facility interaction
2. first harvest
3. manure as a useful resource
4. compost creation/use
5. second/improved harvest
6. mountain route choice/discovery
7. first market sale
8. first village request
9. visible farm/satoyama growth
10. month/season anticipation

### Qualitative success signals
Without prompting, testers say or behave like:
- "I want to see the next month/season."
- "What can I find in the mountain next?"
- "I want to finish that villager's request."
- "The circular system makes sense."
- "This feels different from a normal farm game."

### Failure signals
- "What am I supposed to do?"
- "It's just pressing buttons for numbers."
- "It's relaxing but boring."
- "Why would I open this tomorrow?"
- "It's basically Hay Day/Stardew with different art."

## 9. Quantitative alpha gates

These are initial alpha targets, not industry truths. Recalibrate after the first real cohort.

- FTUE completion: >= 70%
- first harvest completion: >= 90%
- core circular loop completion: >= 60%
- first sale completion: >= 65%
- voluntary month advance after FTUE: >= 50%
- median first session: >= 8 minutes
- severe progression blocker: 0
- save loss / unrecoverable save failure: 0

Retention proof after enough users:
- D1 target for continued investment: >= 30%
- D1 danger: < 20%

Do not optimize D7/D30 until the first-session and D1 sample is credible.
