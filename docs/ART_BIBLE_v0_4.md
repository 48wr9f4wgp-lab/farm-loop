# Farm Loop Art Bible v0.4

## Identity
**雪国の里山 × 循環農業 × 手触りのある暮らし**

Keywords: satoyama / snow-country / handcrafted / warm / seasonal / tactile / calm but alive.

## Palette roles
- Forest: #315A43 / #4F8058
- Young leaf: #7EB35E
- Rice paper: #FFF8E9
- Earth/wood: #8A6444 / #B98A55
- Snow blue: #CFE0E3
- Creek: #6BA9B2
- Honey/reward: #E1B84D
- Hazard: #C9564E
- Ink: #193126

## Shape language
- Terrain: rounded, broad, layered bands.
- Buildings: compact gabled silhouettes, chunky wooden parts, strong readable roofs.
- Vegetation: clustered rounded masses with small irregularity.
- UI: soft rectangles only where information truly needs a panel.

## Material language
Wood / packed earth / rice-paper cream / dark metal / snow / water / leaf canopy.

## Depth & lighting
- Soft upper-left light.
- Short soft shadows under characters/buildings.
- Front objects slightly darker and more saturated.
- Mountains and distant forest lower contrast.

## Facility silhouettes
- Coop: gabled henhouse + tiny fence + moving chicken dots.
- Compost: roofed compost bay + stacked organic layers + subtle steam.
- Sansai: stepped forest-edge beds + leaf clusters.
- Mushroom: stacked logs + canopy + mushroom caps.
- Bee: hive boxes + bees orbiting during active season.

## Motion hierarchy
- Tier 0 ambient: water, smoke, bees/chickens, foliage, snow/rain.
- Tier 1 navigation: 0.1–0.18 sec press response.
- Tier 2 work: 0.3–0.7 sec facility pulse + resource feedback.
- Tier 3 reward: stronger burst + number/title.
- Tier 4 major: short scene-wide accent only.

## Accessibility
- Ready uses sign/shape + glow, never color alone.
- Reduced Motion stops bobbing, drifting particles and looping pulses.
- Critical warnings must use icon/shape/text as well as color.

## Do not
- Do not use emoji as primary facility/navigation art.
- Do not copy benchmark building silhouettes, specific icons, compositions or animations.
- Do not cover the world with permanent panels.
- Do not make every action equally flashy.
