# Farm Loop Entertainment Pivot v0.5

Status: Canonical game-specific direction
Date: 2026-08-23

## Product statement
Farm Loop is not a literal Minami-Uonuma farming simulator.
It is a highly readable, rewarding mobile game about turning a snow-country satoyama into a rich, living place.
Minami-Uonuma, sansai, mushrooms, snow, bees, chickens and village culture provide identity and texture; they must not limit entertainment.

## Fictionalized setting
Use a fictional snow-country region, working name **YUKISATO / 雪里**, inspired by Minami-Uonuma.
This keeps the visual and cultural specificity while allowing game-first events, rare variants, exaggerated harvests, discoveries and progression.

## 60 / 30 / 10 content rule
- 60% universal game pleasure: harvest, discovery, combo, progression, expansion, collection, reward, readable goals.
- 30% snow-country satoyama identity: snowmelt, spring mountain harvest, autumn mushrooms, bees, wildlife, village life, snow-country facilities.
- 10% deep real-world detail: named sansai, cultivation details, composting, biosecurity, habitat notes and regional references.

Realism never wins over clarity, pacing, agency or fun unless realism itself creates an enjoyable decision.

## v0.5 core pillars
1. **Mountain Exploration** — a short tap-driven discovery action with guaranteed useful outcomes and chances for rare finds.
2. **Rare Finds** — memorable named variants and discoveries that are exciting even if the player knows nothing about sansai.
3. **Loop Chain** — chicken -> compost -> month transition -> return compost -> sansai harvest -> sell. Repeating the ecology loop becomes a visible rewarding chain rather than background simulation.
4. **Satoyama Growth** — the map becomes visibly richer as prosperity rises. Progress must be visible in the land, not only in numbers.
5. **Big Reward Moments** — rare finds, completed chains and land-rank ups use high-tier feedback.

## Friction rules
- Exploration never consumes a scarce energy currency.
- The player can never become unable to play because a resource is exhausted.
- First explorations each month are high-value; additional exploration remains possible but has diminishing reward to avoid grind.
- Wrong actions do not destroy a loop chain. They simply do not advance it.
- Regional knowledge is optional. The game explains value through rarity, visuals and reward before lore.

## Presentation rule
The normal farm is calm. Big audiovisual emphasis is reserved for:
- rare discovery
- completed ecology chain
- land-rank up
- level/area unlock
- major sale or mission

## Success definition for v0.5
A new player should be able to say, without knowing the source region:
- "I know what I should do next."
- "I want to see what I find in the mountain."
- "I understand that the chicken/compost/sansai loop makes my land better."
- "I can see my satoyama becoming richer."
- "I want one more rare find / one more chain."
